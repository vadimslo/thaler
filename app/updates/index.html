Changelog

Updates

What changed, when.

  1. v0.2
    2026-09-16
    The app

    The site is now one application: Home, Banks, Auction House, Swap, Activity, Protocol Info. Live ledger of on-chain events, status strip with the epoch clock, hero illustrations. Writes carry a gas floor after two out-of-gas reverts from tight public RPC estimates.

  2. v0.1.1
    2026-09-15
    Withdrawal fee, hardened treasury

    The exit fee now applies to every withdrawal, decaying from 60% at mint to 2% after thirty days; resolution is the final withdrawal. Genesis seed is atomic with pool creation. POL compounding is capped per tick and rate limited. Buyback caps read treasury-owned liquidity only. Allocation rolls epochs first. Accrual is booked per epoch boundary.

  3. v0.1
    2026-09-15
    Testnet launch

    Testnet launch on Sepolia: currency, charters, central bank, flow hook, treasury, router. Six contracts verified on Etherscan. Every parameter published in the whitepaper.
